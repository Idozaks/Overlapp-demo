
# Overlapp – Match Explainer Carousel

## Purpose
This carousel component will visually explain the Overlapp profile building and matching process to new users. Positioned directly under the promotional video on the landing page, it breaks down the journey from creating a digital identity to meaningful connections in an easily digestible format.

## Carousel Slides

### Slide 1: "Digital Identity Formation"
**Headline:** Create Your Digital Identity
**Caption:** Overlapp builds a comprehensive profile based on your interests, cultural background, and identity attributes. This goes beyond typical social profiles to create a more authentic digital representation of who you are.
**Image Placeholder:** `https://source.unsplash.com/random/?digital-identity`

### Slide 2: "Intelligent Matching"
**Headline:** Discover Meaningful Connections
**Caption:** Our two-layer matching algorithm analyzes both shared interests and deeper identity attributes, finding overlaps that matter. The result is more authentic connections based on what truly makes you unique.
**Image Placeholder:** `https://source.unsplash.com/random/?connection-network`

### Slide 3: "Physical & Digital Integration"
**Headline:** Bridge Online & Offline Experiences
**Caption:** Overlapp seamlessly connects your digital profile to real-world interactions. Whether meeting someone at an event or connecting online, your digital identity travels with you, making every encounter more meaningful.
**Image Placeholder:** `https://source.unsplash.com/random/?physical-digital`

## Implementation Notes

### Technical Requirements
- Select an appropriate React carousel library (consider react-slick, swiper, or embla-carousel)
- Fetch placeholder images from an image API until custom illustrations are available
- Implement with mobile-first design principles, ensuring full responsiveness
- Add subtle animations for slide transitions

### Design Guidelines
- Overlay captions on images with semi-transparent gradient backgrounds for readability
- Use consistent typography matching Overlapp's existing brand style
- Include pagination indicators and optional navigation arrows
- Consider auto-rotation with pause on hover
- Maintain accessible contrast ratios for all text elements

### Placement
- Position immediately after promotional video on landing page
- Ensure seamless visual transition from video to carousel
- Consider adding a subtle divider or spacing element between video and carousel
